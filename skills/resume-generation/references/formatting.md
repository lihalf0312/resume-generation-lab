# Preserved formatting and validation requirements

These rules preserve source workflow/format preferences, not a specific applicant's identity. Values requiring an absent template remain unknown. Later explicit user instructions may override scoped rules; record that scope.

## Page and typography

- Each requested language's DOCX and PDF must fill one full portrait A4 page (210 × 297 mm). Body should approach the template body area's lower edge with normal margins and a small safety allowance. A one-page count alone does not pass if a large blank bottom area remains.
- Preserve actual template font families, sizes, margins and page settings. Prefer selection and concise rewriting. Do not shrink fonts without permission, conceal/crop content, inflate font size/line spacing or add large empty lines to force fit or fill.
- Where true relevant evidence is insufficient, report that conflict rather than fabricate filler. Add only supported relevant details, skills or transferable evidence.
- Between adjacent long experiences prefer one blank line when space permits. Short entries may stay compact. Do not add blank lines mechanically to all entries; revalidate page fit and paragraph endings after spacing changes.

## Paragraph endings and alignment

- Every body paragraph's final rendered line must exceed 50% of its available line width; aim for approximately 67–80%. For list items, use the width after indentation. Names, short headings and date fields are not mechanically expanded to meet this prose rule.
- Adjust through accurate rewriting, supported implications/outcomes and detail selection, not meaningless text. Recheck rendered endings; a value outside the preferred band can still satisfy the strict >50% rule.
- Check actual rendered bullet marks and same-level title starts, not just paragraph properties.
- Use `characterSpacingControl=doNotCompress` for punctuation control while preserving other font/page settings. This is separate from automatic East Asian/Latin/number spacing.

## Bilingual and heading details

- Chinese section headings have upper and lower rules; English headings have a lower rule. Preserve each actual language template's distinct top/header layout.
- Bold dates in both languages. In Chinese education, bold the entire graduation-date field, including its label, colon and supplied date. No particular date is bundled.
- A main experience row follows the neutral pattern `Organization | Department-Role`; use only supplied names/titles. Preserve template tab stops rather than spaces for alignment.
- The source formatting preference includes an authorized supplied portrait and political-content field in Chinese and excludes both in English. This is a layout/content-inclusion preference, not a personal attribute: never infer political status or fabricate/request sensitive details unnecessarily. If not supplied/authorized, omit and record the exception.
- Preserve portrait aspect ratio and appearance. No regeneration or beautification without explicit instruction.
- Core bilingual facts correspond. Modest differences in detail, wording or omission for visual fit are allowed, including intentional template differences, provided retained claims remain truthful and nonmisleading.

## Spacing and punctuation

- Keep effective automatic East Asian/Latin and East Asian/number spacing enabled (`autoSpaceDE`, `autoSpaceDN`). Do not manually add spaces around numbers, disable these settings, or introduce special fixes around plus signs. Preserve genuine numeric values, plus signs and original fonts.
- Check paragraph settings and inheritance: absence of an explicit disabling node is not proof the effective option is enabled. Verify actual Word properties when Word is available; otherwise qualify that check.
- English, including across text-run boundaries: one ordinary half-width space between words; one space after commas/colons/semicolons and none before; one space between sentences; one space each side of date-range separators; no extra spaces inside parentheses or around technical slashes.
- Preserve semantic exceptions such as clock times, URLs, thousands separators, technical names (e.g. C++) and normal compound words. Do not mistake justification-generated visual word distances for literal extra spaces. These English normalization rules do not override Chinese automatic numeric spacing.
- Each short education/other-information item that occupies one rendered line has no ending full stop in either language. Do not strip all multi-line prose punctuation. For a local punctuation request, preserve other wording and formatting.

## Verification and restoration

- Render the final DOCX and exported PDF, inspect all pages and compare facts and key numbers with the authorized source. Check page size/count, body utilization, bottom blank area, clipping, overflow, extra breaks, Chinese font fallback, photo proportion, visual alignment and final-line ratios.
- PDF must contain extractable/selectable body text. A full-page image is not an acceptable substitute.
- When restoring a requested version, prefer its exact original and compare files/package members; do not reconstruct and assume equivalence. Reapply only expressly requested changes, then verify.
- Source supplied no numeric margins, fonts, point sizes, bottom whitespace tolerance or renderer. Do not invent those as original requirements. An implementation may declare provisional test thresholds separately, clearly distinguishing them from this contract.
